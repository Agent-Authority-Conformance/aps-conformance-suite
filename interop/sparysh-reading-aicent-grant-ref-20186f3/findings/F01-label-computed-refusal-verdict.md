# F01 — Section 8.1: what verdict does a refusal computed over a label earn?

To: the author of draft-li-rttp-iqa-addressing-02
Cases: GR-A01 (blind B13). Draft only; not filed.

Two adjacent sentences of 8.1 point to different verdicts for a refusal whose value is right but whose recorded basis is an operator label:

- "For the same reason, a refusal reason MUST be computed over the authority facts the response carries — facts a third party can re-derive from pinned inputs." Read alone, this makes the response nonconformant.
- "A refusal computed over labels, opinions, or unrecorded context alone is not auditable, and producing one is not a conformance target of this section." Read alone, this places the response outside what 8.1 judges.

The independent reading scored this case `ambiguous`, as the candidate does, and the parts match. The word "alone" adds a third point: when a label is one input among authority facts, neither sentence clearly applies.

Possible clarification: state whether such a response is nonconformant, or out of scope, and whether "alone" is intended.
